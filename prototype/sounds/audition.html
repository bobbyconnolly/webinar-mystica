Loudness is RMS over the whole file in dBFS; peak is the bar (red past -1 dBFS after gain). The footstep roles play four times at walking cadence so you hear what repetition sounds like. Export writes sounds-manifest.json: file, role, gain, rating, source, notes. Put chosen files under prototype/sounds/ and the manifest beside them; the game applies the gains at play time.
