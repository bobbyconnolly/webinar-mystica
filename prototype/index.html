Shading

Movement

Everything that blocks is a height. Step up to 0.4, jump the rest; walls (2.6) never. Fence 0.8, crate 0.6, table 0.9. Slopes over 45° block, and loose earth slumps to 38°, so only stone-faced banks stop anyone. Water slows past 0.4 deep and blocks past 1.2 without the skill. Deer hop fences, sheep don't.

Sculpt

Left drag applies the tool, right drag always lowers. Disturbed earth steeper than the angle of repose slumps until it is stable. Stone facing holds a slope. Nothing ever reverts on its own.

Town

The ward is a lobe: short behind the town, long ahead. Pale stones mark the threshold. Digging inside brings guards; near the fringe they take longer. Lower the strength to watch warded land shrink from the edges in.
